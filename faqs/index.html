Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about KS Garden Services. Whether you need regular maintenance or a one-off garden tidy-up, I'm here to help!

General Questions

What areas do you cover?
I'm based in Newton-le-Willows and cover Warrington, St Helens, Ashton-in-Makerfield and surrounding areas. If you're unsure whether I cover your location, please get in touch.
Do you offer free quotes?
Yes! I provide free, no-obligation quotes tailored to your garden and requirements. Photographs are helpful when making an initial enquiry. Some jobs may require a visit before I can provide a quotation.
Do you offer regular maintenance?
Yes. I offer weekly, fortnightly and other scheduled maintenance visits, subject to availability. Services include lawn mowing, edging, weeding, hedge cutting and general garden upkeep.
Can I book a one-off tidy-up?
Absolutely! Whether your garden needs a seasonal tidy-up, hedge cutting or general attention, I'm happy to provide a quotation.

Appointments & Access

Do I need to be home?
For your first appointment, it's preferable that you're home so we can discuss your requirements and arrange access. Regular maintenance can usually be carried out while you're away, provided safe access has been arranged.
What happens if it rains?
A little rain doesn't usually stop me! However, if conditions are unsafe or could damage your lawn, I'll contact you to rearrange your appointment.
Do I need to provide equipment?
No. I bring my own professional gardening equipment, so you don't need to supply any tools.
What if I have pets?
Pets are absolutely fine! Please ensure your garden is clear of dog and cat waste before my arrival. Pets must be kept safely away from the areas where I'm working.

Pricing & Payments

How much does maintenance cost?
Every garden is different. I provide individual quotations based on garden size, work required, access and visit frequency. You'll receive a clear quotation before work begins.
How can I pay?
I accept bank transfers and card payments. Payment details will be provided with your invoice.
Do you remove garden waste?
Yes! Garden waste removal can be arranged for an additional charge. Where possible, I'm happy to use your existing green waste bin to help keep costs down.

Booking & Availability

How far ahead should I book?
I recommend booking early, particularly during spring and summer. I'm currently taking enquiries for the 2027 gardening season.
How do I book?
Contact me through the website, WhatsApp or email. Tell me about your garden and the work required. I'll discuss your requirements and arrange a visit if necessary. Once you're happy with your quotation, we can arrange your first appointment.

Still Have Questions?

I'm always happy to discuss your gardening requirements. Contact me today for a free quote!
