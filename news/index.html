Seasonal advice, useful reminders and updates from KS Garden Services.

5 October 2026

October garden update

Keep lawns clear of fallen leaves, tidy tired herbaceous growth selectively and use the cooler months to plan hedge work and bigger reductions.

5 October 2026

Plant of the month: ivy

Ivy is often cut back hard, but where it can be left safely to flower it provides valuable late nectar for bees and other insects. Its berries later provide food for birds.
