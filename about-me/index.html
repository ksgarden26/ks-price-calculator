A LITTLE MORE ABOUT ME

The story behind KS Garden Services

A few questions customers often ask about me and the business.

How did you first get into gardening?

Karl: It really started in childhood. I’ve always enjoyed being outdoors and working with gardens, plants and practical jobs.

What made you start KS Garden Services?

Karl: After more than 10 years working in an office environment, I wanted to build something for myself around an industry I genuinely enjoy. Gardening felt like the right fit.

What do you enjoy most about the work?

Karl: Pretty much everything. I enjoy seeing the difference a good tidy-up, fresh lawn cut or properly shaped garden can make.

What makes KS Garden Services different?

Karl: I try to keep things simple — be friendly, reliable, pay attention to detail and treat every customer's garden with care.

What does your family mean to the business?

Karl: A lot. I’m a husband and a proud dad, and my family is one of my biggest motivations for wanting KS Garden Services to succeed.

I want to build something I can be proud of and create a successful business for our future. They’re part of the journey with me, even if I’m the one you’ll normally see behind the mower!

What are your ambitions for KS Garden Services?

Karl: To keep growing the business while maintaining the personal service customers expect. I want KS Garden Services to become a trusted local name for reliable garden care.

Simple, reliable garden care.
