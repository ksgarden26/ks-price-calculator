Voucher terms

Clear and simple.

  • Vouchers are valid for eligible services provided by KS Garden Services.
  • Work is subject to availability and normal service-area coverage.
  • The voucher must be presented or quoted when booking.
  • Vouchers are non-refundable and cannot be exchanged for cash.
  • The expiry date is shown on the voucher.
  • If the job value is greater than the voucher value, the remaining balance is payable by the customer.
  • Normal KS Garden Services terms and conditions apply to booked work.
