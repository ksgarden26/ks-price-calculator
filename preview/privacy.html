Privacy

How KS Garden Services handles information submitted through this website.

Information you provide

When you request a quote or send an enquiry, you may provide your name, address, postcode, phone number, email address, garden details and photographs.

Why it is used

This information is used to respond to enquiries, prepare and manage quotes, arrange work, keep customer records and provide the services you request.

Website enquiries

Enquiries submitted through the website may be transferred into the KS Garden Services customer/quote tracking system so they can be followed up and managed.

Cookies and analytics

Optional analytics or marketing technologies will not be loaded until you have chosen to allow them. You can change your choice at any time using the Cookie settings link in the footer.

Website reviews and responses

If you submit a review, your chosen display name, star rating, review text and any owner response are published on our website. An optional email address is kept privately and is never displayed with the review. If you choose the separate email-reply option, it may be used to notify you of a response.

Review information is stored in the website’s Cloudflare database. Notification emails are sent using a separate email delivery provider when enabled. To request access, correction or removal of personal information relating to a review, contact info@ksgardenservices.co.uk. Basic abuse prevention may involve short-term use of hashed technical identifiers.

Contact

For privacy questions, email info@ksgardenservices.co.uk.

This page is a practical website privacy notice and should be reviewed whenever the systems used by KS Garden Services change.
