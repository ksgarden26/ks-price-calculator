About KS Garden Services

A local garden-maintenance business built around reliability, care and attention to detail.

Hi, I’m Karl.

Gardening has been part of my life since childhood. After more than 10 years working in an office environment, I decided to build something around an industry I genuinely enjoy.

KS Garden Services is about helping customers keep their gardens tidy, healthy and cared for without making things complicated. I aim to be friendly, dependable and clear about what a job involves.

My focus is on regular garden maintenance, lawn care, hedges and shrubs, seasonal work and selected one-off services such as pressure washing.

What matters to me

✓ Turning up when agreed
✓ Leaving gardens neat and tidy
✓ Clear communication
✓ Building long-term customer relationships
