THIS MONTH

October garden checklist

October is about tidying with purpose rather than stripping everything back. Clear heavy leaf fall from lawns, keep paths safe, plant spring bulbs, check stakes and ties, and leave useful seed heads and shelter where wildlife can use them.

  • Clear wet leaves from lawns and hard surfaces
  • Plant spring-flowering bulbs
  • Give hedges a final tidy where appropriate
  • Aerate and scarify lawns where conditions allow
  • Check greenhouse ventilation and clean glazing
  • Leave some seed heads, ivy flowers and wildlife shelter
KS tip

A tidy garden does not have to be a bare garden. Keep structure and habitat where it is useful, and remove what is likely to cause problems.
