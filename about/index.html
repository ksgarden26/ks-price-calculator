About KS Garden Services

A local service built around care and reliability.

I’m Karl, the person behind KS Garden Services, providing practical garden maintenance with a personal approach and attention to detail.

Gardening has been part of my life since childhood. After more than 10 years working in an office environment, I decided to build something around an industry I genuinely enjoy.

I now work professionally in garden maintenance and run KS Garden Services for local customers, with a focus on being friendly, reliable and paying attention to the details that make a garden feel properly cared for.

What matters to me

I want customers to know who is turning up, what the job will cost and what is included. I’m building the business around long-term relationships rather than rushed visits.
